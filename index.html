AboutUs

The students of Web DnD are a diverse and accepting crowd! Each student brings a unique background and a unique skill set. Each one has mastered something different, and together they make an excellent team.

We are Web Design & Development!

Students today, professionals tomorrow.

Our students design brands, build websites, and host them for real clients. Every project is a real job, from the first meeting to launch day.

Along the way they learn what it takes to work with a client: listening, presenting ideas, taking feedback, and delivering a finished site that people actually use.

WhatWe Do

Students of Web Design & Development are multi-talented. They learn everything from designing a website mockup to writing its content, digital art, coding, and much more!

Designing

Design starts on day one. We begin in Adobe Illustrator and build from there. By the end of the program, students have learned Illustrator, Photoshop, and more.

Developing

Every student learns HTML, CSS, and introductory JavaScript, then builds real sites with WordPress and Divi. In their third year they take the NOCTI, a proctored state performance exam.

Marketing

The first thing students learn to market is themselves! We use LinkedIn to polish our digital footprint, and social media marketing is now part of the curriculum.

Branding

Logos should be SIC: Simple, Iconic, and Consistent. Students critique each other's work as they build complete brands, with style tiles that define a corporate identity.

How ItWorks

Hiring a class is a little different from hiring an agency. Here's what to expect, from first message to launch.

  1. 01

    Reach out

    Tell us about your project using the form below or by email, and we'll follow up to talk through what you need.

  2. 02

    Design

    Students sketch ideas and build mockups. You review the designs and share feedback before anything gets built.

  3. 03

    Build

    Once the design is approved, students build your site, often on WordPress with Divi so it's easy to update later.

  4. 04

    Launch

    Your site goes live, and we can host it on the BackStacks server with an SSL certificate included.

FAQs

Questions & Answers

Who actually builds my site?

Students in the Web Design & Development program at BAVTS, working under the guidance of their instructor.

How long does a project take?

It depends on the size of the project and the school calendar. We'll give you an idea of timing when we first talk.

Can I update the site myself?

Many of our sites are built on WordPress with Divi, which makes editing text and images straightforward. Let us know if that matters to you when you reach out.

What happens after the students graduate?

If you host with us, your site stays online on the BackStacks server for as long as your hosting is renewed.

What's included in hosting?

Hosting is $150 a year and covers hosting on the BackStacks server with an SSL certificate included.

Do you only build websites?

No. We also design logos, brand identities, website artwork, and photography.
