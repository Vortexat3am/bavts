What We Do

Students of Web Design & Development are multi talented. They learn everything from designing a mock up of a website to the content to fill it with, digital art, coding, and so much more!        

  • Designing

    Students begin learning design their very first day of class. Design is the first step of any project. We start with Adobe Illustrator and build from there. By the completing the program the students shoudl have learned Illustrator, XD, Photoshop, and more.

  • Developing

    All students learn HTML, CSS, and intoductory JS. In their third year they take a proctored state performance exam called the NOCTI in these languages. Students may use the code editor of their choice to develop. Many students choose to add python to add to their skillset.

  • Marketing

    The first thing they learn to market is themselves! We use linkedin to polish our digital footprint and add as much positivity as possible. These days we are what one can google. That is why the emerging trend of social media marketing was just added to the curriculum.

  • Branding

    The fundamental concept of branding is that logos should be SIC, Simple, Iconic, and Consistent. They work together to critique work Throughout their time in class as they create many brands complete with style tiles to define a corporate identity.
